Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 May 2026

1. Introduction

Expense Guru is a personal finance and expense tracking mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use Expense Guru.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect account information such as name, email address and login credentials; financial tracking information entered by you, including expenses, income, transfers, budgets, categories, payment modes and notes; app settings such as preferred currency, language and profile preferences; and technical information required for app functionality, security, diagnostics and service improvement.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide expense, income, transfer and budget tracking features.
  • Show spending summaries, budget insights and category analysis.
  • Improve app reliability, security and user experience.
  • Respond to support, privacy or account deletion requests.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may use trusted infrastructure or service providers only when required to operate the app, such as hosting, database, authentication, analytics or diagnostics services.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user data. Data transmitted between the app and server should be protected using encrypted connections such as HTTPS.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and app data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and enforce our terms.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Expense Guru account and associated data through our account deletion page: Expense Guru Account Deletion Request.

8. Children’s Privacy

Expense Guru is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with the updated date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at support@expguru.ai.
