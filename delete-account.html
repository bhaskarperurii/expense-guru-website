Expense Guru Account Deletion Request

Use this page to request deletion of your Expense Guru account and associated app data.

Expense Guru provides both in-app account deletion and this web-based deletion request option for users who no longer have access to the app.

In-app account deletion

You can delete your account directly inside the Expense Guru mobile app.

  1. Open the Expense Guru app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

If you no longer have access to the app, you can still request deletion by emailing us using the instructions on this page.

How to request account deletion

If you want to delete your Expense Guru account and associated data, email us from your registered email address.

Email: support@expguru.ai

Subject: Delete My Expense Guru Account

Include: Your registered email address and a short confirmation that you want your Expense Guru account deleted.

Data that may be deleted

  • Account profile information
  • Email-linked account data
  • Expense records
  • Income records
  • Transfer records
  • Budget records
  • Category budget records
  • App settings linked to your account

Data that may be retained

We may retain limited information only when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or compliance reasons. Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

In-app deletion option

You may also request deletion inside the Expense Guru app if the option is available in your installed version. If you no longer have the app installed, use the email process on this page.

Contact

For deletion or privacy questions, contact support@expguru.ai.
